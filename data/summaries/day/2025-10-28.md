On October 28, 2025, Sendo's development focused on core API enhancements, including data analysis and rate limiting, alongside essential maintenance for plugins and a new frontend feature. The `sendo-api` saw significant improvements in data handling and stability, while `plugin-cryptoscore` and `plugin-sendo-analyser` received dependency and version updates. A new feature for worker-code implementation was also initiated in the frontend.
