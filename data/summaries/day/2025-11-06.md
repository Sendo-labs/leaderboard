On Nov 6, 2025, the Sendo project saw significant refactoring efforts in the `frontend` repository, focusing on standardizing color variable management for consistent visual identity and refining the storage codebase for improved maintainability and type safety.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Frontend Refactoring & Standardization
- Standardized color variable management across the application by replacing hardcoded hex values with CSS variables for Sendo brand colors, introducing `sendo-dark-red`, and updating gradient backgrounds, ensuring consistent visual identity. ([Sendo-labs/frontend#25](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend/pull/25))
- Refactored the storage-related codebase to standardize naming conventions, enhance type safety, and modernize the factory implementation, improving maintainability and reliability. ([Sendo-labs/frontend#24](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend/pull/24))
