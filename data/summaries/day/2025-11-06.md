On November 6, 2025, the Sendo project saw significant progress in the frontend repository, primarily focusing on code quality and maintainability through extensive refactoring. Key achievements included standardizing color variable management across the application and refining the storage-related codebase for improved consistency and type safety.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Frontend Refactoring & Standardization
- Standardized the usage of brand colors throughout the application by replacing hardcoded hex values with CSS variables, introducing `sendo-dark-red`, and updating gradient backgrounds across various components in [Sendo-labs/frontend#25](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend/pull/25).
- Refactored the storage-related codebase to standardize naming conventions, enhance type safety, and modernize the factory implementation, including renaming files and interfaces and updating related actions and adapters in [Sendo-labs/frontend#24](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend/pull/24).
