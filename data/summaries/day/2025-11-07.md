On November 7, 2025, Sendo's development focused on enhancing the `plugin-sendo-analyser` with asynchronous wallet analysis and improving the `frontend`'s storage capabilities through a new abstraction layer, alongside a critical bug fix. These updates collectively aim to boost the platform's analytical power and operational stability.

## ✅ Completed Work
### Frontend Enhancements & Bug Fixes
- **Enhanced Storage Abstraction**: A significant feature was added to abstract storage providers, introducing adapters for AWS Parameter Store, AWS Secret Store, Local Parameter Store, and Local Secret Store, allowing for flexible and scalable secret and parameter management. ([Sendo-labs/frontend#26](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend/pull/26))
- **Critical Bug Fixes**: A bug related to "0x Canet" was addressed, impacting various components across the application, ensuring smoother operation and stability of the frontend. ([Sendo-labs/frontend#23](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend/pull/23))
