# Overall Project Weekly Summary (Oct 26 - 1, 2025)

This week, Sendo made significant strides on two parallel fronts: delivering major user-facing features for AgentAI interaction and analysis, while simultaneously overhauling our development infrastructure to accelerate future innovation. We launched a new agent chat interface and integrated a powerful trade analyzer, all supported by a massive investment in automated testing, deployment, and release workflows across multiple projects.

### Key Strategic Initiatives & Outcomes

**Accelerating Development with Foundational Automation**
*Strategic Importance: By automating our testing and release processes, we reduce manual effort, increase reliability, and enable our team to ship new features faster and more consistently.*
-   Automated release and publishing workflows were established across several key plugins, including `plugin-solana` ([#1](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-solana/pull/1)), `plugin-jupiter` ([#2](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-jupiter/pull/2)), and `plugin-sendo-worker` ([#3](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-worker/pull/3), [#5](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-worker/pull/5), [#6](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-worker/pull/6)), culminating in the first automated v1.0.0 release for the Sendo Worker.
-   A comprehensive testing infrastructure with 98 automated tests was implemented in `plugin-sendo-worker` ([#2](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-worker/pull/2)), creating a strong foundation for stability and future development.
-   Work began to bring this same level of automation to the `plugin-sendo-analyser` ([#8](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser/pull/8)), standardizing our development lifecycle.

**Launching the Next-Generation AgentAI Experience**
*Strategic Importance: These initiatives directly advance our mission of empowering investors by providing them with secure, intuitive, and powerful tools to analyze their history and create their own agents.*
-   The user experience was transformed in the `frontend` ([#10](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend/pull/10)) with the integration of a robust authentication system, providing secure session management for our users.
-   A new, interactive `AgentChat` component was launched in the `frontend` ([#13](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend/pull/13)), replacing the previous interface to make interacting with AgentAIs more intuitive.
-   The core wallet analysis engine was migrated into `plugin-sendo-analyser` ([#1](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser/pull/1)), creating seven new API endpoints that power our analysis features. This new capability was then successfully integrated into the `frontend` ([#17](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend/pull/17)).

**Fortifying On-Chain and Backend Infrastructure**
*Strategic Importance: Strengthening the security and reliability of our core systems is critical for building user trust and ensuring our platform can scale effectively.*
-   The on-chain auction program in `spotlight` ([#1](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/spotlight/pull/1)) was significantly hardened with new admin-only verification and time validation rules to enhance security and prevent misuse.
-   A rate-limiting mechanism was implemented in the `sendo-api` ([#6](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/sendo-api/pull/6)) to protect the service from excessive traffic and ensure stability for all users.
-   The trade processing logic in `sendo-api` was refactored for better modularity and maintainability ([#7](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/sendo-api/pull/7)), making our backend easier to scale and improve.

**Automating Open-Source Contributor Rewards**
*Strategic Importance: This initiative directly supports our core philosophy of open collaboration by creating a transparent and scalable system to reward community contributors.*
-   The new `leaderboard-rewards` repository was launched with the creation of an oracle bot designed to automatically fetch contributor XP and synchronize rewards ([#1](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/leaderboard-rewards/pull/1)), laying the groundwork for a decentralized community incentive program.

### Cross-Repository Coordination

This week highlighted strong coordination between our frontend and backend teams to deliver a major new feature. The migration of core analysis logic into `plugin-sendo-analyser` directly enabled the `frontend` team to integrate the new trade analyzer feature, demonstrating a successful end-to-end execution. Furthermore, a project-wide initiative to standardize development workflows saw four separate repositories—`plugin-solana`, `plugin-jupiter`, `plugin-sendo-worker`, and `plugin-sendo-analyser`—adopt similar CI/CD automation pipelines, a key step towards improving modularity and scalability across the entire Sendo ecosystem.
