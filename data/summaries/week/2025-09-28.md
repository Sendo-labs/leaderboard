# Overall Project Weekly Summary (Sep 28 - 4, 2025)

## Executive Summary (2-3 sentences)
This week marked the expansion of the Sendo ecosystem with the launch of the new `leaderboard` project. Initial development focused on establishing the foundational infrastructure for tracking and recognizing contributor activity, setting the stage for future analytics capabilities that align with our philosophy of open collaboration.

### Key Strategic Initiatives & Outcomes

**Establishing a Foundation for Community Analytics**
This initiative is the first step toward building a system that provides analytics and recognizes contributor activity, a key part of fostering a vibrant and open community.
-   The project kicked off with the creation of the [Sendo-labs/leaderboard](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/leaderboard) repository and the implementation of its initial contributor tracking system.
