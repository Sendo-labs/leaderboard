# Overall Project Weekly Summary (Nov 23 - 29, 2025)

## Executive Summary
This week, development focused on initiating two foundational initiatives aimed at strengthening our platform. We began a comprehensive overhaul of the user interface to create a more modern and intuitive experience, while simultaneously starting work to enhance the core on-chain analysis engine for greater accuracy. These parallel efforts set the stage for a more powerful and user-friendly platform.

### Key Strategic Initiatives & Outcomes

**Building a More Intuitive and Modern User Interface**
This initiative is crucial for making our platform accessible and easy to use, allowing every investor to interact with their AI agents seamlessly and effectively.
-   Work has begun on a complete refactor of the application's user interface and experience in [Sendo-labs/frontend](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend), kicking off a major effort to modernize the platform's design.

**Enhancing the Intelligence of Our Trading Agents**
To deliver on our promise of smarter trading, our agents must interpret complex on-chain activities with high precision. This work directly improves the quality of the data our AI relies on.
-   We started to refine our on-chain data analysis capabilities in [Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser), with initial efforts focused on improving how swap transactions are processed.

## Repository Spotlights

### Sendo-labs/frontend
-   Initiated a significant, foundational refactor of the entire user interface and user experience to modernize the platform and improve usability ([#31](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend/pull/31)).

### Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser
-   Began work to enhance on-chain data analysis by improving how swap transactions are filtered and processed, aiming for greater accuracy in agent insights ([#23](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser/pull/23)).
