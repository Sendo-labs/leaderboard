# Overall Project Monthly Summary (December 2025)

## Executive Summary
December was a month of foundational strengthening, focusing on preparing the Sendo platform for future growth. Efforts were concentrated on enhancing the project's legal framework to build user trust and refining the core analysis engine for greater accuracy and stability.

### Key Strategic Initiatives & Outcomes

**Strengthening Platform Compliance and User Trust**
This initiative ensures our platform operates transparently and meets necessary legal standards, which is critical for building a trustworthy relationship with our users.
- Essential legal documentation, including the privacy policy, cookie policy, and terms of service, was integrated directly into the user interface, making it easily accessible to all users ([Sendo-labs/frontend](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend)).

**Improving Core Analysis and System Stability**
This work focuses on making our trade analysis engine more accurate and ensuring our software components work together seamlessly.
- Work began on a significant overhaul of the core trade analysis logic to improve the accuracy and efficiency of its calculations ([Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser)).
- An update was initiated to improve compatibility with standard software packages, ensuring smoother integration and long-term stability for the analysis plugin ([Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser)).

## Repository Spotlights

### Sendo-labs/frontend
Development focused on establishing the platform's legal and compliance framework.
- New pages for the cookie policy, privacy policy, and terms of service were created and linked in the application's footer, ensuring users have clear access to important legal information ([#35](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/frontend/pull/35)).

### Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser
This month's efforts centered on foundational code improvements to enhance the accuracy and compatibility of the trade analysis engine.
- Work was initiated to refactor the core trade analysis logic, aiming to improve metric parsing and the accuracy of global summary calculations ([#25](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser/pull/25)).
- A pull request was opened to fix ESM import extensions, a technical change that improves compatibility with standard npm packages and simplifies dependency management ([#24](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/plugin-sendo-analyser/pull/24)).
