# Sendo-labs/sendo-api Weekly Report (Oct 19 - 25, 2025)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week's development was centered on enhancing the stability and reliability of the API's core trading functionalities. Efforts began with an important documentation update to improve accessibility for a global audience. The remainder of the week was dedicated to a series of critical bug fixes, addressing issues in buy/sell account handling, data retrieval pagination, and service integrations. These fixes have significantly improved the robustness and accuracy of transaction processing and data retrieval within the Sendo API.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
Work this week focused on bug fixes and documentation, strengthening the foundation of the API.

### Core Trading and Account Handling Fixes
A significant effort was made to resolve bugs impacting the reliability of trading operations. A critical bug in the buy/sell account functionality was addressed, involving comprehensive changes to the `trades.ts` controller, `birdeyes.ts` service, and utilities for balance extraction and rate limiting ([#3](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/sendo-api/pull/3)). Further refinements were made to improve how buy and sell accounts are handled and to update related service integrations ([#4](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/sendo-api/pull/4)).

### Data Retrieval and Pagination Improvements
To ensure more accurate and reliable data fetching, a critical bug in the API's pagination logic was resolved. This fix involved modifications to the `trades.ts` controller and the `helius.ts` service, correcting how data is retrieved in paginated sets ([#5](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/sendo-api/pull/5)).

### Documentation and Accessibility
To better serve a global community, the project's `README.md` was updated with a full English translation. This enhancement improves clarity and accessibility for international contributors and users ([#2](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/sendo-api/pull/2)).

## 🐛 Issues & Triage
- **Closed Issues:** No issues were closed this week.
- **New & Active Issues:** No new issues were opened, and there were no active issues with significant discussion reported during this period. The development focus remained on implementing fixes via pull requests.

## 💬 Community & Collaboration
Development activity this week was characterized by focused, direct contributions. The work was completed through a series of pull requests without extensive public discussion noted in GitHub issues or PR comments. There was no reported activity from new contributors.
