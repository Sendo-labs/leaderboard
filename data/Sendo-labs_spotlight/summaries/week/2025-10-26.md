# Sendo-labs/spotlight Weekly Report (Oct 26 - 1, 2025)

## 🚀 Highlights
This week's development was centered on a significant push to enhance the security and reliability of the `sendo_spotlight` on-chain program. The core auction settlement and SOL transfer mechanisms were substantially improved to ensure proper fund handling and prevent unauthorized actions. A key part of this effort was the introduction of comprehensive unit tests, establishing a strong foundation for future development and ensuring the stability of the platform's core functionality.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
All of the week's work was consolidated into a single, comprehensive update aimed at making the auction process more robust and secure.

- **Enhanced Auction Settlement and SOL Transfer Logic**
  - A major update in PR [#1](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/spotlight/pull/1) focused on robustifying the on-chain program.
  - **New Features & Security:** The logic for settling auctions was enhanced with administrative verification, restricting settlement to the auction creator. Time validation was also added to prevent auctions from being settled outside their designated window.
  - **Refactoring & Performance:** SOL transfer methods were refined, implementing Cross-Program Invocation (CPI) for user-to-vault transfers and direct lamport manipulation for vault-to-recipient transfers to improve security and efficiency.
  - **Testing:** To validate these critical changes, a comprehensive suite of unit tests was developed and integrated. These tests cover various scenarios including user-to-vault and vault-to-recipient transfers, admin verification, and time-based auction validation.
