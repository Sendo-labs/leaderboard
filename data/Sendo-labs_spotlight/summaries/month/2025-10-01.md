# Sendo-labs/spotlight Monthly Report (October 2025)

## 🚀 Highlights
October's development was characterized by a focused effort to strengthen the core auction functionality of the `sendo_spotlight` program. The primary achievement was a significant enhancement of the SOL transfer and auction settlement logic, aimed at improving security and reliability. Key updates included implementing administrative verification for settlements, adding time validation for auctions, and refining fund transfer mechanisms. This foundational work was supported by the introduction of a comprehensive unit testing suite to validate the new, more robust logic.

## 🛠️ Key Developments
Work this month was concentrated on a single, impactful update to the core program's security and reliability.

- **Enhanced Auction Settlement and SOL Transfer Logic**
  - To ensure secure and efficient fund handling, development focused on robustifying the auction process. This involved implementing proper Cross-Program Invocation (CPI) for user-to-vault SOL transfers and using direct lamport manipulation for vault-to-recipient transfers.
  - Security was further tightened by introducing administrative verification, which restricts the ability to settle an auction to its original creator.
  - Time validation was also added to prevent auctions from being settled prematurely or after their designated end time.
  - This effort included the creation and integration of a comprehensive unit testing suite to validate the new transfer and settlement mechanisms, significantly improving the program's stability. All of these critical updates were delivered in pull request [#1](https://github.com/Sendo-labs/spotlight/pull/1).
